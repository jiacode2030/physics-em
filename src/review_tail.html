Exam format

AP Physics C: Electricity and Magnetism · May 2027 exam · 3 hours total

Section I: Multiple choice
42 questions · 85 min · 50%
Four answer choices each. About 2 minutes per question.
Section II: Free response
4 questions · 95 min · 50%
One of each type:
  • Mathematical Routines
  • Translation Between Representations
  • Experimental Design and Analysis
  • Qualitative/Quantitative Translation
How you take it
Hybrid digital. You answer multiple choice in the Bluebook app. Free-response questions appear in Bluebook, and you handwrite your answers in a paper booklet.
Calculator
Allowed on both sections: an approved scientific or graphing calculator, or the Desmos calculator built into Bluebook.
Reference sheet
An equation and constants sheet is provided for both sections.
What changed
For May 2027, multiple choice went from 40 questions in 80 minutes to 42 in 85, and free response went from 100 to 95 minutes. The four question types and the 50/50 split are the same.
AP Physics 2
Same layout for May 2027: 42 multiple-choice questions in 85 minutes, then 4 free-response questions in 95 minutes, with no calculus required. Check the AP Physics 2 exam page.
