Five ideas you'll use everywhere

  • Fields carry the force. A charge makes a field; the field pushes other charges: F = qE.
  • Superposition. Fields from separate sources just add, as arrows.
  • Voltage is height. Positive charges roll downhill in potential, like balls on a hill.
  • Moving charge makes magnetism, and magnetism pushes sideways on moving charge.
  • Changing flux makes EMF. That one idea runs generators, transformers and induction stoves.

Suggested path

AP Physics C: E&M: go in order, Units 8 to 13. Maxwell's equations is an optional wrap-up. Then work through FRQ practice.

AP Physics 2: do the modules tagged AP 2: Coulomb's law, fields, potential, capacitors, circuits, magnetic force, Ampère's law (the field of a wire) and Faraday's law. Skip the calculus-heavy ones.

Before an exam: drill Which tool?, build a mixed set in Exam review, and clear your mistakes queue.

Shaky on the integrals? The optional Math toolkit shows how to turn a charged rod into an integral, and how exponential curves work.

Stuck on notation? Open the Formula sheet button at the bottom right.
