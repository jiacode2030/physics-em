Which tool do I use?

Half the battle in E&M is choosing the right law. Learn the clues, then practice picking.

The intuition

Each law answers a different kind of question. Coulomb's law is the brute-force way to add up charges. Gauss's law is the shortcut when the charge has symmetry. Potential and energy make problems about speed and work easy because they are scalars. Kirchhoff's rules handle circuits. Magnetic force laws handle moving charges and currents. Biot–Savart and Ampère's law find magnetic fields, and Faraday's law handles anything that changes with time.

Ask first: is the question about a field, a voltage, a current in a circuit, a force from B, or something that changes?

Clue in the problemReach for
A few point charges; asks for force or field at a spotCoulomb's law and superposition (draw each arrow, add components)

A circuit with batteries and resistors; asks for currents or voltagesKirchhoff's rules (junction and loop), plus series/parallel shortcuts
A capacitor or resistor changing over timeKirchhoff's loop rule with a time constant: exponential charge or decay

A changing flux, moving rod or loop, or a changing current; asks for EMF or induced currentFaraday's law, with Lenz's law for direction

Practice: pick the tool

Choose the first tool you would reach for.

1. Find the electric field 2 cm from the center of a uniformly charged solid sphere.

2. Three point charges sit at the corners of a triangle. What is the net force on one of them?

3. A proton is accelerated from rest through 500 V. How fast does it end up?

4. Find the current in each branch of a circuit with two batteries and three resistors that aren't simply in series or parallel.

5. An electron moves at 3 × 10⁶ m/s perpendicular to a 0.01 T field. What is the radius of its path?

6. What is the magnetic field 4 cm from a long straight wire carrying 10 A?

7. A rod slides along rails in a magnetic field. What EMF is induced across it?

8. Find the magnetic field at the center of a circular arc of wire carrying current.

9. A capacitor charges through a resistor. What is the current after 2 time constants?

10. How much work does it take to move a +2 nC charge from a point at 10 V to a point at 60 V?
