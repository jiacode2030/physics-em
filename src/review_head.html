How to earn points

  • Start from a fundamental equation (Gauss's law, Kirchhoff's rules, Faraday's law, a definition) before substituting.
  • "Derive" means show the steps symbolically. Write the answer in terms of the given symbols, not numbers.
  • Directions need reasons. For a current or force direction, name the rule you used: Lenz's law, a right-hand rule, the sign of the charge.
  • "Justify" or "explain" needs physics reasoning, ideally pointing back to an equation or principle. A correct choice with no reason usually earns nothing.
  • Later parts are often graded against your earlier answers. If (b) is wrong but you use it correctly in (c), you can still earn (c)'s points, so keep going.
  • Graphs: label axes, mark intercepts and asymptotes, and get the shape right (exponential approach, 1/r, linear).
