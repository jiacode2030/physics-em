Maxwell's equations

Four equations contain everything about electricity and magnetism. Maxwell noticed one was missing a term, added it, and out popped a wave traveling at 3 × 10⁸ m/s: light is an electromagnetic wave.

The intuition

You have already met each of the four laws. Gauss's law says electric field lines start and end on charges. Gauss's law for magnetism says magnetic field lines never start or end: no magnetic monopoles. Faraday's law says a changing magnetic field makes an electric field. Ampère's law says currents make magnetic fields.

Maxwell realized Ampère's law was incomplete. Charge a capacitor: current flows in the wire, but no charge crosses the gap between the plates, yet the magnetic field circling the wire has to be the same size whether the loop is drawn around the wire or through the gap. His fix: a changing electric field also makes a magnetic field. The extra term, ε₀ dΦE/dt, is the displacement current.

Now the two curl laws feed each other: a changing B makes E (Faraday), and a changing E makes B (Maxwell). That is a self-sustaining wave, with each field regenerating the other as it travels. Plug in the constants and the speed comes out as c = 1/√(μ₀ε₀) = 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s: the speed of light. Light, radio waves and X-rays are all the same thing at different frequencies.

While the capacitor charges, a real current I flows in the wire but no charge crosses the gap. Yet B around the gap is just as if it did: the changing E between the plates acts like a current. Maxwell called it the displacement current, and it is what lets electromagnetic waves exist.

Try this

Predict what will happen first. Then set it up and check.

  1. Slide the frequency to 100 MHz (FM radio) and read the wavelength.

    What you should see

    λ = c/f = 3.0 m. The wave is a radio wave. The speed is the same for every setting; only the wavelength and frequency change.

  2. Go up to visible light (about 5 × 10¹⁴ Hz).

    What you should see

    The wavelength is about 600 nm, a few hundred nanometers. Light is an electromagnetic wave with a frequency millions of times higher than FM radio.

  3. Displacement current: pick the point between the plates at r = 2 cm.

    What you should see

    There is no real current in the gap, yet there is a magnetic field. It is smaller than the field at the same distance around the wire (the displacement current only fills part of the loop) and grows in proportion to r.

  4. Move the point outside the plates (r = 8 cm).

    What you should see

    Now the loop encloses all the displacement current, which equals the real current I. B in the gap equals B around the wire: the two sides of the capacitor give the same B.

Worked example

A radio station transmits at 100 MHz. At a certain point the electric field amplitude of the wave is E₀ = 50 V/m. Find (a) the wavelength, (b) the amplitude of the magnetic field, (c) the intensity (average power per area) and (d) the direction of travel if E points in +y and B points in +z.

Try it yourself first, then reveal one step at a time.

  1. Wavelength. λ = c/f = (3.0 × 10⁸)/(1.0 × 10⁸) = 3.0 m.
  2. Magnetic field amplitude. In an electromagnetic wave E0 = cB0, so B0 = 50/(3.0 × 10⁸) = 1.7 × 10⁻⁷ T.
  3. Intensity. I = ½ε0cE0² = ½(8.85 × 10⁻¹²)(3.0 × 10⁸)(50)² = 3.3 W/m².
  4. Direction. The wave travels along E × B: ŷ × ẑ = x̂, so it moves in the +x direction.
  5. Check. The energy is shared equally between the electric and magnetic fields: ½ε0E² = B²/(2μ0) at every instant ✓.

Common mistakes

  • Thinking E and B in a wave are 90° out of phase (like a pendulum). They peak together.
  • Forgetting that the wave moves along E × B, not along E or B.
  • Believing the displacement current is a flow of charge. It is a changing electric field.
  • Using c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s in a medium: in glass or water the wave moves slower.

Electromagnetic waves

  • Speed: c = 1/√(μ0ε0) = 2.998 × 10⁸ m/s, in vacuum, for every frequency.
  • Relations: c = fλ, E = cB at every point, and the energy flow (Poynting vector) is S = E × B/μ0 along the direction of travel.
  • Spectrum: radio, microwave, infrared, visible (400–700 nm), ultraviolet, X-rays, gamma rays: the same wave at different frequencies.

Displacement current

  • Between capacitor plates of area A: Id = ε0 dΦE/dt = ε0A dE/dt = dQ/dt, equal to the real current in the wires. For a loop of radius r inside the plates, B = μ0Ir/(2πR²).
